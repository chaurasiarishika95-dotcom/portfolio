The voice bot: key product decisions

  • AI understands, rules decide. Gemini reads what the caller says; fixed rules control access, fees, refunds and every action. The model never cancels anything by itself.
  • No action without a clear yes. Before cancelling, the bot reads back the fee and refund and waits for confirmation.
  • Role-based access. An employee sees only their own bookings; a central booker can manage the team's.
  • Quality before scale. Release gates on the eval set, with critical errors (wrong cancellation, wrong booking shown) held at zero.

What I'd look at first at MakeMyTrip

  • Which post-booking intents drive the most contacts today, and which of them the bot can resolve safely end to end.
  • Where self-service journeys (cancellation, date change, refund status) lose customers to a call, measured as contact rate per booking.
  • How handoff reasons split between "the bot couldn't" and "the bot shouldn't", since only the first group should shrink.
