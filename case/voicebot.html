Voicebot: AI post-booking travel assistant

A voice assistant that answers booking questions, cancels safely and hands complex cases to a person, with quality measured before every release.

Problem

Most post-booking calls to a travel desk are repetitive: booking details, refund status, cancellations. They take agent time, but a bot that cancels the wrong booking or shows someone else's trip is worse than no bot.

Users

Corporate travellers checking or cancelling their own trips, central bookers who manage team travel, and travel desk agents who pick up the hard cases.

My role

Defined the product and wrote the PRD (v1.1 and v1.2), built the prototype with AI tools, designed the test set and ran the A/B test of fixes.

Key decisions

  • AI reads the caller's words; fixed rules decide access, fees, refunds and every action. The model never cancels anything on its own.
  • Nothing is cancelled without reading back the fee and refund and getting a clear yes.
  • Callers only see bookings their role allows (employee vs. central booker).
  • North Star = safe autonomous resolution rate, not containment, so a caller who gives up never counts as a win.
  • Every handoff carries a ticket (reason, booking, what was quoted, frustration causes), so the caller never repeats themselves.

How I tested it

  • Built a 153-case golden set: paraphrases, Hinglish, wrong bookings, access attempts, fee disputes.
  • Grouped the failures into patterns, made 6 fixes and compared old vs. new on the same cases: pass rate rose from 84% to 95%, unneeded handoffs fell from 17 to 1.

Known gaps

  • Voice runs in the browser (Chrome speech), not on a phone line; real deployment needs telephony with low latency and interruption handling.
  • Bookings, fares and fees are sample data; production needs a GDS or booking-tool integration.
  • Next version: date changes with fare re-check and travel-policy limits, and Hinglish replies.

Tools

Gemini (Flash-Lite with model fallback and a rule-based fallback), browser speech recognition and text-to-speech, Vercel serverless.
