Case study

AI Visa Assistant (Cognizant)

An internal assistant for recurring business-visa questions, taken from a hackathon prototype to rollout.

~60 → 15 minVisa process time (75% faster)
86%Accuracy across 1,500 test cases
2,000Historical support tickets analysed

Problem

Employees waited about an hour for answers to recurring business-visa questions that the mobility team answered again and again.

My role

Analysed 2,000 historical support tickets to find the most common query types, prototyped the assistant on Lovable during a company GenAI hackathon to win business buy-in, then owned it as the business analyst through rollout: user stories, acceptance criteria and coordination with the Engineering and AI team, who built it.

Key decisions

  • Extend the existing Q&A chatbot into a RAG-based, action-oriented assistant instead of starting from scratch.
  • Defined business rules and guardrails: what it should ask, answer, refuse or escalate, plus the fallback between action and Q&A modes.

How it was tested

Designed and ran 1,500 test cases to catch hallucinations, wrong retrievals and guardrail failures, fed failures back to the AI team, and reached 86% accuracy before rollout. Applied DMAIC to cut visa process time from about an hour to 15 minutes.

Note

Internal company system: no public demo or documents. Details are limited to what can be shared.
